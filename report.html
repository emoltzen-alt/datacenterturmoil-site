National Trends

Local resistance to data center development remains widespread and bipartisan: roughly 7 in 10 Americans say they oppose an AI data center being built near them, and Data Center Watch estimates over $64 billion in projects have been blocked or delayed by community opposition nationwide, with $130B+ delayed or canceled in Q1 2026 alone. About a dozen states have floated moratorium bills, and New York and Texas have already enacted temporary pauses. The core resident complaints are consistent across the map: strained water supplies, rising electricity bills, constant industrial noise, light pollution, traffic, and skepticism over tax abatements that shift cost onto homeowners. An Aug. 8 NPR piece noted the issue is now surfacing directly in state races, with candidates in both parties — from Wisconsin's Francesca Hong to Ohio's Vivek Ramaswamy — calling for moratoriums.
