Data Center Community Response — Daily Report

24-hour window: Aug 1, 2026 13:00 UTC – Aug 2, 2026 13:00 UTC · Background items drawn from the preceding ~3 weeks for national context
National trends: Local opposition to data center development continued to intensify heading into August, with recurring themes of packed public hearings, moratorium votes, and planning-commission rejections across Virginia, North Carolina, Iowa, Maryland, Kentucky, Ohio, Colorado, Kansas, Texas, Missouri, and beyond. Communities cite water use, grid strain/electricity costs, noise, and loss of local control (NDAs, secrecy) as top concerns. Genuinely new developments inside the strict last-24-hour window were limited — mainly procedural (new hearings scheduled) rather than new votes or protests — so this report keeps the "Current" section short and honest rather than padding it, while the "Older / Background" section documents the broader wave of activity from the prior few weeks.
