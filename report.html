Today's national picture. Local resistance to data centers reached a new scale over the last 24 hours: HumansFirst, a conservative-leaning advocacy group, organized the first nationally coordinated day of action against data centers on Saturday, July 18, with roughly 142 separate protests reported across 42 states — Texas (18 events), Georgia (11) and California (8) saw the most activity. The demonstrations channeled the same complaints driving the past several weeks of council and commission fights nationwide: rising electricity and water costs, noise and light pollution, farmland loss, and a sense that deals are struck behind closed doors before residents get a say.
