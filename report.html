National Trends Summary

Opposition to data center development remains high and increasingly procedural: while public hearings still draw large, vocal crowds over water use, noise, electricity costs and property values, the newest tactic on both sides is litigation — cities suing residents to block citizen petitions (Edgerton, KS) and residents/tribes suing over environmental review. Moratoriums continue to spread (Fort Worth TX, Louisville KY, Lakeland FL, Alamance County NC, Imperial Valley CA in the past two weeks alone), and state legislatures are now the venue of choice for grid and transmission-line fights, as shown by Wednesday's nine-hour, overflow-crowd hearing at the Texas Capitol.

In the past 24 hours specifically, activity centered on a Texas legislative hearing on data centers and 765kV transmission lines, a Kansas city suing its own residents over a data center ballot petition, a contentious final-approval vote in a Pennsylvania township, and a Michigan town hall where nearly all speakers opposed future development near a former Air Force base.
