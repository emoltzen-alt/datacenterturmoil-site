National Trends

Moratoriums keep spreading (Mesa County CO approved a 12-month pause; Guilford NC, Beaufort NC and Buffalo NY are moving on similar measures), while other bodies decline to regulate (Grady County OK, Okaloosa FL) or approve projects over objections (Red Oak TX). Residents most often cite water, grid strain and power rates, noise and transparency. Cities in Kansas are suing residents who petition against projects.
