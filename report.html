Data Center Community Response — Daily Report

24-hour window: Sept 21, 2026 1:15pm UTC – Sept 22, 2026 1:15pm UTC · Generated Sept 22, 2026

National Trends

Local pushback is escalating from meeting-room objections into binding policy: Texas Gov. Greg Abbott ordered a statewide pause on new data-center environmental permits pending a water/power/grid audit, while Prince William County, VA — the nation's largest data-center market — held a hearing on rolling back its by-right overlay district. Kansas's Lawrence-Douglas County Planning Commission added a special-use-permit requirement, and Hancock County, IN closed out a resident advisory committee's siting report. Not every recent vote goes against developers, though: Spotsylvania County, VA's planning commission approved a new mega-campus over neighbors' objections. Across nearly every case, the same concerns recur — water consumption, electricity rates and grid strain, industrial noise, lost farmland/tax questions, and complaints that zoning changes were rushed with too little public notice, several of which have already moved into lawsuits and one to a state Public Service Commission docket.
