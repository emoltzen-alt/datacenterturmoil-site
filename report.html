Perryville, Missouri Current
Board of Aldermen · ordinance revision considered · September 1, 2026

Aldermen took up a third, toughened revision of the city's data center ordinance — even though no project has been proposed for Perryville. The draft requires a public hearing before any license, a 500-foot buffer from homes and vulnerable-care facilities, a 50,000-gallon-per-day water cap, closed-loop cooling, a ban on groundwater wells and once-through cooling, and environmental/noise impact assessments.
