National trends

Local resistance to data center development keeps hardening rather than fading: moratoriums, lawsuits, and packed public hearings are now a weekly occurrence across red and blue states alike, driven by consistent complaints — water and power strain, constant mechanical noise, rising utility and property-tax bills, and a felt loss of local control when siting decisions move fast relative to public notice. In the past 24 hours, a fifth-largest-metro county (Durham, NC) joined the growing list of jurisdictions pausing new projects, a contested air-quality permit fight in New Mexico escalated to the state Supreme Court, and Texas's capital city moved to write its first dedicated data center zoning rules — evidence the fight is shifting from ad hoc resident pushback toward more permanent regulatory frameworks in city and county code.
