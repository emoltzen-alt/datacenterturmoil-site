National Trends Summary

  • Moratorium wave continues. In just the past two weeks, counties from Washington State to Georgia to California (Spokane, Cherokee, Alamance, Coweta, Calvert, Snohomish, Tulare, and more) have passed or extended pauses on new data center construction, typically ranging 45 days to a full year, almost always following standing-room-only public hearings.
  • Litigation is becoming the second front. Beyond zoning fights, developers and opponents are increasingly suing each other — over open-meetings and public-notice violations (Joliet, IL), constitutional claims against local bans ($300M suit in Monroe Township, NJ), and disputes over citizen referendum petitions (Edgerton, KS).
  • Recalls and referenda are the newest tactic. Voters in Festus MO, Claremore OK, Augusta Township MI, and Port Washington WI have filed or advanced recall/referendum petitions against officials who approved data center projects — a sign the fight is moving from planning commissions to the ballot box.
  • Consistent resident concerns nationwide: water consumption and water pressure/quality, electricity rate hikes and grid strain, constant low-frequency noise from cooling/generators, farmland and property-value loss, and skepticism that promised tax revenue and jobs offset these costs.
