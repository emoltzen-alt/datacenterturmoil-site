National Trends Summary

Local opposition to data center development continues to accelerate nationwide, with moratoriums now the dominant tool: Louisville (KY), Bellevue (WI), Waxhaw (NC), Fort Worth (TX) and dozens of other jurisdictions have enacted or advanced construction pauses in the past two weeks alone. Litigation is a fast-growing second front — residents and developers alike are suing over open-meetings violations, referendum handling, and moratorium legality in Ohio, Missouri and Kentucky. The Ohio Supreme Court's ruling clearing an Ashville, OH referendum to proceed (today) signals courts are increasingly willing to check councils that fast-track approvals. Recurring resident concerns across nearly every hearing: water consumption/scarcity, electricity rate increases, 24/7 industrial noise, loss of rural/agricultural land, and tax breaks perceived as one-sided. Several communities (Bellevue WI, Waxhaw NC) are passing preemptive moratoriums before any project has even been proposed.
