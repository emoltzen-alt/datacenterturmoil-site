Data Center Community Response — Daily Report

24-hour window: July 27, 2026 – July 28, 2026 · U.S. local community response to data center development

National Trends Summary

The moratorium wave continues to broaden: at least 15 states now have county-, city-, or Tribal-level pauses on data center permitting, with roughly six new or advancing moratoriums logged in the past week alone (Cherokee, Coweta, Chesapeake, Sierra, Yankton, Spokane). Opposition is increasingly organized and litigious — Data Center Watch now counts 430+ local opposition groups across 40 states, and lawsuits challenging "minor development" classifications (Spartanburg County, SC) are becoming a recurring tactic to force full public review.

Within the past 24 hours specifically, the center of gravity shifted from moratorium votes to public hearings on standing proposals: Salem, OR and Louisville, KY both held marathon public-comment sessions on large pending data centers, while in Texas the fight moved up a level to a state Senate committee reviewing tax incentives and oversight statewide. Recurring resident concerns nationwide remain consistent: water and power demand, noise from cooling systems, property values, and skepticism that promised tax revenue offsets rising utility bills.
