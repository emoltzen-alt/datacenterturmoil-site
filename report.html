Today's national picture. Local resistance to data centers keeps hardening into policy: Good Jobs First now counts at least 63 local moratorium actions nationwide with 54 already passed, and nearly a third of Indiana's counties alone have adopted some form of restriction. Data Center Watch estimates $64 billion in projects have been blocked or delayed by community opposition. The through-line across today's items is the same as prior weeks — water and Colorado River allocation fights, rising electricity bills (78% of Americans tell Consumer Reports they're worried), noise complaints, and skepticism that promised tax revenue offsets the costs. The moratorium/pause remains the dominant municipal tool, but outright rezoning denials (Prince William County) and voter-enacted bans (Monterey Park) are becoming more common endpoints.

Prince William County (Gainesville), Virginia Current
Board of County Supervisors · vote · July 7, 2026

Supervisors voted 8-0 to reject the comprehensive plan amendment for the "Dulles South Innovation Center" (Dulles Cloud South), a proposed 43-million-sq-ft, 1,940-acre data center campus near the Loudoun County border. About 100 protesters gathered outside the McCoart Building and roughly 95 residents spoke during the nearly six-hour hearing, citing water, power infrastructure, and rural-character concerns; county staff itself had recommended denial.

Nashville (Davidson County), Tennessee Current
Metro Council · public hearing · July 7–8, 2026

Metro Council held a marathon public hearing on paired zoning-restriction bills and a temporary permitting moratorium for data centers, using a ticketed queuing system for the first time as 245+ residents signed up to testify by 8 p.m. Opposition centers on a DC BLOX project proposed near the Nashville Zoo affecting historically Black North Nashville neighborhoods; Mayor Freddie O'Connell publicly called DC BLOX's "collaborative talks" characterization "inaccurate, wishful thinking." All three bills advanced on second reading, with a final vote expected in two weeks.
