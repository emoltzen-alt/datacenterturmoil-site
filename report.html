Data Center Community Response — Daily Report

24-hour window: July 26–27, 2026 (with recent background context) · Generated July 27, 2026

National trend: Local resistance to data center development continued to intensify this week. Community groups now actively oppose an estimated 1,500+ planned facilities across 49 states, and Data Center Watch puts the value of projects blocked or delayed by local opposition at roughly $64 billion. Moratoriums remain the fastest-growing tactic — new pauses landed in South Dakota, New Mexico, Wisconsin, Maine, Florida and Michigan in the past two weeks alone — while a handful of jurisdictions (Edgerton, KS; Colorado Springs, CO) instead pushed contested projects through over planning-commission or resident objections. Water use, noise from cooling/generators, electricity-rate impacts, and skepticism over promised tax revenue remain the consistent flashpoints nationwide.

On the 24-hour window specifically: confirmed, freshly-dated news events landing precisely in the prior 24 hours were limited. The clearest ones are noted below; most of the week's substantive votes, hearings and filings are slightly older (roughly the past 1–2 weeks) and are grouped in the Background section rather than padded into "current."
