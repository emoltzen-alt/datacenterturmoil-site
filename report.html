Data Center Community Response — Daily Report

24-hour window: Sep 18, 2026 (afternoon) – Sep 19, 2026 · Compiled Sep 19, 2026

National Trends

Local pushback against data center development remains widespread and largely procedural: this window's genuinely new activity is concentrated in zoning and permitting mechanics — a postponed generator-variance hearing in Pennsylvania, a proposed moratorium extension in Illinois, and a council signaling openness to smaller facilities in California — alongside a fresh noise and environmental-justice complaint from an already-burdened Dallas neighborhood.

The broader backdrop (see Older/Background) shows the same pattern repeating nationwide: county and town boards are split roughly evenly between granting moratoriums/rejections and approving projects after contentious hearings, with water use, noise, electricity costs, and transparency of the approval process as the recurring resident complaints. Litigation is an emerging track alongside zoning fights, with class-action noise suits and lawsuits challenging approval processes filed in multiple states over the past two months.
