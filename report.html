Data Center Turmoil — Daily Report

Window: 24 hours ending 2026-10-10 13:13 UTC · Interactive map

National Trends

Moratoriums keep spreading: Raleigh, Beaufort County NC, Brighton CO, Anne Arundel MD and Lexington KY all acted in the past week, and Memphis is set to take up a 12-month pause Oct 20. Meanwhile approvals continue over loud objection (Taylor TX, Casa Grande AZ), often followed by lawsuits. Recurring fears: water, power bills, noise, and inadequate public notice. Counties such as Nye NV choose rules short of bans, leaving some residents unsatisfied.
