National Trends

Local opposition to data centers continued to intensify nationally this week. Community pushback is now bipartisan and increasingly organized: moratorium votes, rezoning denials, and lawsuits are being filed at a pace unmatched earlier in 2026, with an estimated $130B+ in projects blocked or delayed so far this year. Recurring flashpoints:

  • Water & power strain — cited in nearly every hearing, from California to Virginia to Texas.
  • Moratorium wave — counties in Georgia, Ohio, Oregon, Pennsylvania, Illinois, and Kentucky moved on temporary bans or studies in the past week.
  • Ballot & legal fights — voters and residents are increasingly turning to referenda (Michigan, Ohio) and lawsuits (Illinois, Texas, Utah) after losing at the board level.
  • Escalating tone — protest theatrics (a guillotine at a Salem, OR hearing), police responses, and arrests were reported at multiple meetings this week.
