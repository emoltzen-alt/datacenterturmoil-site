Yellowstone County, Montana Current
Board of County Commissioners · letter to state legislature · September 26, 2026

Commissioners Mark Morse, Mike Waters, and Chris White approved and sent a letter Tuesday asking the county's legislative delegation to "refine" Montana's 2025 Right to Compute Act, which they say strips the county of authority to regulate siting, permitting, and zoning for Quantica Infrastructure's proposed 5,000-acre data-center campus near Broadview. The move follows weeks of public outcry, and Broadview resident Kassi Solberg continues organizing a ballot initiative that would require two-thirds voter approval before any data center could operate in the county.

Sources: Daily Inter Lake — "After weeks of public outcry, Yellowstone County asks state to clarify 2025 data center law" · Montana Free Press
Pierce County, Nebraska Current
Planning & Zoning Board · 7–1 recommendation vote · September 26, 2026

The county's planning and zoning board voted 7–1 to recommend a six-month moratorium on new data-center applications, giving officials time to study water, power-grid, noise, and tax impacts before any formal applications are accepted. Public testimony was overwhelmingly supportive of a pause, with several residents arguing six months isn't long enough. The recommendation now goes to the Pierce County Board of Commissioners for a final decision expected in mid-to-late October.

Sources: Norfolk Daily News — "Regional roundup for Sept. 26" · Pierce County Journal
Memphis, Tennessee Current (≈Sept 25)
City Council · ordinance amendments detailed · reported September 25, 2026

City leaders outlined amendments to a proposed one-year moratorium on new large-scale data-center permits, including a companion expert task force to study impacts on Memphis Light, Gas & Water's power grid and local water supply. The ordinance's final council reading is set for October 6; the Greater Memphis Chamber has urged a "no" vote, warning a pause could cost the city future projects following its recent SpaceXAI deal.
