Data Center Community Response

Window: Oct 2–3, 2026 (prior 24 hours) · compiled 2026-10-03

National trends

Local pushback continues to center on moratoriums (Bangor, Palm Springs, Oakland, Indianapolis, Fort Worth), packed public hearings (Somerset County and Forward Township, PA), and resident fears about water, power, noise and process transparency. At the state level, New Hampshire's governor says she will propose a moratorium. Texas counties face state pressure against moratoriums.
