National Trends

  • National opposition to new local data centers has risen sharply: roughly 61% of U.S. adults now oppose construction in their area, up 12 points since a Feb/Mar survey — including majorities of Democrats (69%), Republicans (54%) and independents (53%).
  • Opponents blocked or delayed at least 75 projects worth about $130 billion nationwide from January–March 2026, the most in any three-month period since tracking began in 2023; active local opposition groups have more than doubled to 833 across 49 states.
  • About a dozen states have proposed data center moratoriums this year; New York and Texas have already enacted temporary bans, and dozens of counties and cities have passed or advanced local pauses.
  • Recurring resident concerns nationwide: electricity rate increases, water consumption/scarcity, 24/7 industrial noise, property values, and skepticism that tax breaks are worth the tradeoffs given low permanent headcount.
