Today's national picture. Local resistance to data center development continues to spread and intensify across red and blue states alike, with moratoriums now the default first move for city councils and county commissions facing new proposals. Water use, electricity costs/grid strain, noise, and property-tax giveaways remain the four recurring flashpoints; a growing number of fights are moving from council chambers into courtrooms (restraining orders, referenda, and direct lawsuits against local governments) as residents lose confidence that public hearings alone will change outcomes. National tracking projects (Data Center Watch, datacenterbans.com) now put well over 300 municipalities on record with bans or pauses and tens of billions of dollars in projects delayed or blocked.

The Lakes at Marshall Ridge HOA and neighboring Keller homeowners sued, citing noise, power and water demands, and property-value concerns from the 88-acre, 1.1-million-square-foot project.
