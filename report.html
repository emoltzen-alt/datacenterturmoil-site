Today's national picture. The moratorium remains the dominant municipal response nationwide — at least a dozen counties and cities have adopted or extended a data-center development pause in the past two weeks alone (Newberry County SC, Davie County NC, Pasco County FL, Lawrence KS, St. Charles County MO, Imperial County CA, Santa Fe County NM, Washington County MD, Walker County GA, among others).
