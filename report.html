Data Center Community Response — Daily Report

24-hour window: July 21, 2026 – July 22, 2026 · Updated July 22, 2026

National Trends

The moratorium wave keeps widening: nearly a third of Indiana counties have now moved to restrict data centers, New York's governor is set to sign the country's first statewide hyperscale moratorium, and this past day alone saw new or advancing moratoriums in Yankton County, SD, Sierra County, NM, and a second-reading vote in Madison County, FL. Data Center Watch estimates roughly $64 billion in projects have been blocked or delayed nationwide amid local opposition, and denials (Douglas County GA, Naperville IL, Palm Beach County FL, Fauquier County VA, Prince William County VA) continue to outpace approvals in several states.

Across nearly every hearing, the same resident concerns recur: water consumption in drought-prone regions, 24/7 industrial noise and light pollution, strain on the local power grid and electricity rates, opaque or fast-tracked approval processes, and skepticism that promised tax revenue offsets the impact. A national day of protest against AI data centers was held July 18, organized in part by the advocacy group Humans First, reflecting how local fights are increasingly networked into a broader movement.
