Barber County (Sharon), Kansas Current
County Commission · 2-0-1 vote · Monday, Aug. 31, 2026

Commissioners voted 2-0-1 (one abstention) to finalize an agreement approving Google's first Kansas data center, an air-cooled 1.7-million-square-foot facility near Sharon dubbed "Project Helium." The project is projected to generate roughly $10 million a year in county property taxes; residents were split, with the agreement barring water cooling and imposing strict noise limits in response to concerns.

Middlesex Township, Pennsylvania Current
Board of Supervisors · vote postponed · Monday, Aug. 31, 2026 (rescheduled to Sept. 2)

Supervisors postponed a vote — for the second time — on six of the sixteen planned buildings of the proposed PAX-1 data center after the township solicitor suffered a medical emergency mid-meeting and was taken away by EMS. An earlier August meeting had already been postponed after more than 150 residents packed the building, exceeding capacity. Neighbors cite noise, traffic, lighting, and environmental concerns tied to the roughly five-year construction timeline.

The county-wide civic federation voted to formally oppose the 34-acre Plaza 500 data center campus and an associated Dominion Energy substation on Edsall Road, citing a resource protection area bordering Turkeycock Run and a data-center building planned just 67 feet from the nearest home. The move adds a prominent county-wide voice to a fight already drawing in members of Congress and city officials from neighboring Alexandria.

Source: FFXnow — "Fairfax County civic group votes to oppose Plaza 500 data center, substation"
Kokomo, Indiana Current
Common Council · 6–2 vote · Monday, Aug. 31, 2026 (final vote Sept. 14)

The Common Council advanced a zoning ordinance allowing data centers on industrial sites, rejecting calls for a moratorium. The measure sets 200–300 foot setbacks from residences but adds no new rules on utility use, noise, or infrastructure; eleven residents spoke against it during public comment, criticizing the lack of quality-of-life safeguards.

Source: WRTV — "Kokomo council advances data center ordinance despite public opposition" · WISH-TV
