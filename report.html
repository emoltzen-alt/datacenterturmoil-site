Today's national picture. Local opposition to data centers remains broad and bipartisan, with the same core grievances driving nearly every local fight: who pays for grid and water upgrades, noise from cooling and backup generators, and the pace/scale of proposed campuses relative to local review capacity. Moratoriums remain the dominant municipal tool, but a growing share of communities are now litigating — either developers suing over denied permits and expired moratoriums, or residents and advocacy groups suing over noise, unpermitted equipment, and environmental permitting.

Commissioners deadlocked 3–3 on a text amendment that would have effectively banned data centers in unincorporated Bulloch County; Chairman David Bennett's tiebreaking vote killed the outright ban. The county's existing moratorium, in place through Dec. 31, 2026, is unaffected.
