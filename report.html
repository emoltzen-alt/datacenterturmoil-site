Data Center Community Response — Daily Report

24-hour window: July 28, 2026 → July 29, 2026 (plus recent background). Compiled from web research.
National trends: A wave of local moratoriums is spreading — Hillsboro, OR enacted a surprise 120-day pause; Montgomery County, MD passed an 18-month moratorium it says will be harder for developers to challenge; McCormick County, SC is drafting one after resident backlash. Confrontation is escalating: a Doña Ana County, NM commission meeting on the Oracle/OpenAI-linked "Project Jupiter" campus ended in a scuffle and three arrests, and Louisville's mayor was publicly booed by activists for the second time this month. Recurring resident concerns nationwide remain consistent — strain on scarce water supplies (Utah's Great Salt Lake, Goochland County, VA), generator and cooling noise (Denver, Dowagiac, MI), and anger at being blindsided by approvals before local rules exist (Humboldt, TN; Louisville, KY). Rejections and the lawsuits that follow them (Urbana, OH; Prince William County, VA) continue to define the standoff between developers and communities.
