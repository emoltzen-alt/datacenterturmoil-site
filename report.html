National Trends

Local pushback continues to center on electricity rates, water use, noise and farmland. Moratoriums are the dominant tool (Anne Arundel MD, Imperial CA, Beaufort NC, Calvert MD, Elkhart IN), while some boards still approve projects over loud opposition (Casa Grande AZ, McCormick SC). Tax revenue is the usual reason for approvals; harm-versus-benefit is the usual reason for denials.
