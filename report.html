National trends

Local opposition to data center development kept accelerating this cycle. Tracking sites now count more than 300 moratoriums or restrictions enacted across roughly 32 states since 2024, with the pace of new pauses, denials, and lawsuits continuing through early September. Commentators including RealClearPolitics described the backlash as a "September surprise" cutting across party lines, echoing earlier reporting on Missouri and Kansas towns uniting against lawmakers and big tech.

The recurring resident complaints remain consistent nationwide: strain on local water supplies and electricity rates, construction and operational noise (including low-frequency/infrasound concerns), loss of farmland or open space, opaque approval processes (NDAs, closed-session votes), and skepticism that promised tax revenue offsets the costs. Several communities — Independence, MO chief among them — have seen the fight spill into local elections, with council members who backed data center tax breaks losing their seats.
