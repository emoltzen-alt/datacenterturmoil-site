Today's national picture. Opposition to data centers keeps widening and stays bipartisan: this cycle's dominant themes are electricity-bill increases (PJM-region capacity costs up sixfold year over year), water use, noise from turbines/generators/HVAC, and skepticism of developer tax-revenue promises. Moratoriums remain the go-to municipal tool, but a growing share of towns are moving past pauses into permanent zoning ordinances (setback, decibel, and cooling-method rules), and litigation is emerging as a second track once permits are already granted.
