Data Center Turmoil: Daily Report

Window: 24 hours ending 2026-10-08 13:27 UTC. Compiled from web search results.

National Trends

Moratoriums are spreading (Oakland adopted one; Memphis is fighting over one; Baltimore County, Iron County MO and others already have them). Residents increasingly sue over notice and process (Twiggs GA, Wilmington OH) and cite water, power bills, noise and short notice. Approvals still pass in places like Casa Grande and Statesboro, often over loud opposition.
