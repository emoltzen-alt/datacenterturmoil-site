St. Louis, Missouri Current
Lawsuit filed · Board of Adjustment · reported August 29, 2026

Residents, the Missouri Workers' Center, and the Missouri Coalition for the Environment sued the city, its Board of Adjustment, and developer David Lambiaso over a $3 billion data center proposed near the former Armory site in Midtown. The suit argues the Board lacked legal authority to grant a conditional-use permit for a use not recognized in the zoning district, and cites fears of higher local temperatures, air and water pollution, and construction/operational noise.

Sources: St. Louis Public Radio — "Opponents of St. Louis data center take their fight to court" · KMOV First Alert 4 — "Residents, organizations file lawsuit against proposed Midtown data center"

Hope / Hempstead County, Arkansas Current
Public meeting announced · reported August 29, 2026

A public meeting on a proposed 130,000-sq-ft data center in the Hope Industrial Park (on 89 acres, needing roughly 40 MW of electric service) was announced for Hempstead Hall after residents pushed for more transparent information. Coverage this week has questioned developer VINCO's claims of very low water use (800–1,000 gallons/day, closed-loop cooling) and whether the 25 promised $80K-salary jobs would actually be local hires.

Imperial County, California Older
Court ruling · reported August 28, 2026

An Imperial County Superior Court judge set aside the county's emergency data center moratorium, ruling officials failed to show a "current and immediate threat" to public health, safety, or welfare as state law requires. The county said Friday it "respects the court's ruling" and is reviewing next steps; the moratorium had followed months of public outcry over a large proposed desert data center complex.

Sources: KPBS — "Judge poised to strike down Imperial County's data center moratorium" · The Desert Review — "Imperial County reviews next steps after court sets aside data center moratorium"
Indiantown / Martin County, Florida Older
Village council vote · reported August 28, 2026

Indiantown's council voted unanimously to hold a September 1 special meeting on a possible data center moratorium, after residents warned that four separate data center proposals have moved through the village's process in rapid succession. An attorney for one applicant, Silver Fox 606, said the company believes any moratorium would be improper and intends to challenge it legally.

Source: WQCS — "Indiantown sets special meeting to discuss data center moratorium"
