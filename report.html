Today's national picture. Local pushback against data centers continues to accelerate, with county and city boards across the country moving simultaneously on moratoriums, ordinances, and zoning rollbacks. Data Center Watch reported this week that roughly $68 billion in data-center projects were blocked or delayed by local opposition in the second quarter of 2026 alone — nearly $200 billion disrupted so far this year — with 843 organized opposition groups now active in 49 states. Recurring resident concerns remain water use, electricity rates, noise from cooling systems and gas turbines, and loss of farmland/rural character.

Genuinely new in the last 24 hours: Prince William County, Virginia's Board of Supervisors voted unanimously to shrink its data center overlay district and end countywide by-right approvals — a landmark reversal in the state with the nation's largest data-center footprint. Fayette County, Ohio and Wakulla County, Florida both enacted new restrictions (a moratorium and an outright ban, respectively) within the past two days. Iowa's Hancock County tabled a proposed moratorium while neighboring Marshall County moved toward a formal vote, and Woodbury, Minnesota's city council is taking up its own moratorium ordinance today.

Prince William County, Virginia Current
Board of Supervisors · vote · September 22, 2026

The Board voted unanimously to adopt a zoning text amendment shrinking the county's Data Center Opportunity Zone Overlay District from about 9,700 acres to 3,500 acres, effectively ending by-right data center development countywide going forward. Chair Deshundra Jefferson called an unusual mid-hearing closed session for legal guidance before the final vote.

Source: InsideNoVa — "Prince William supervisors vote to end by-right data center development countywide"
Fayette County, Ohio Current
Board of Commissioners · vote · September 21–22, 2026

Commissioners approved a data center construction moratorium running from Sept. 21, 2026 through June 1, 2027, covering seven unincorporated townships. Commissioner Donnie Fleak reversed his initial opposition after hearing public objections at the meeting.

Source: Record Herald — "Fayette Co. commissioners approve temporary data center moratorium"
Wakulla County, Florida Current
Board of County Commissioners · vote · September 21, 2026

Commissioners voted unanimously to adopt an ordinance explicitly prohibiting data centers in all unincorporated areas of the county, formalizing a ban that staff said already existed in practice since data centers weren't a permitted use in any zoning district.

Source: WCTV — "Wakulla County commissioners approve ordinance prohibiting data centers"
Hancock County, Iowa Current
Board of Supervisors · tabled · September 22–23, 2026

Supervisors tabled a proposed data center moratorium, splitting over whether to instead fast-track a permanent ordinance; Supervisor Florence "Sis" Greiman was the lone vote favoring an immediate pause. The move follows the county Planning & Zoning Commission's unanimous rejection of a rezoning request for a data center near Garner earlier this month.

Source: Radio Iowa — "Proposed data center moratorium tabled in northern Iowa county"
Marshall County, Iowa Current
Board of Supervisors · scheduled vote · September 23, 2026

Supervisors reached consensus on language for a formal six-month data center moratorium resolution (through March 23, 2027) and planned to take it up for adoption at today's meeting, following a unanimous recommendation from the county Zoning Commission.

Source: Times Republican — "Supervisors discuss six-month data center moratorium, plan to vote on resolution at next meeting"
Woodbury, Minnesota Current
City Council · scheduled vote · September 23, 2026

The City Council is considering an interim ordinance that would halt new data center applications, related building permits, rezonings, and conditional use permits for up to 12 months while staff studies regulations, citing growing developer interest and the absence of data-center-specific zoning rules.

Source: Woodbury News Net — "Woodbury Proposes Data Center Moratorium Ahead Of Sept. 23 Council Meeting"
