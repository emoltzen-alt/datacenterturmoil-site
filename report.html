Colorado Springs, Colorado Current
Planning Commission · appeals denied 6–2 · hearing concluded July 24, reported July 25, 2026

After a 14-hour special hearing that ran past midnight, the Colorado Springs Planning Commission voted 6–2 to deny five resident appeals of the administratively approved "Project Taurus" AI data center — a Raeden-developed facility planned for the former Intel Microchip site off Garden of the Gods Road. The commission attached conditions including a 50-megawatt power cap, 10 years of utility-use reporting, and low-frequency "infrasound" noise monitoring. Opponents cited power draw, noise, and the site's proximity to residential neighborhoods; the decision can still be appealed to City Council within 10 days.

Source: SoCo Digest — "Project Taurus Appeals Denied After 14 Hour Hearing" · also CPR News, Colorado Springs Gazette
Edgerton, Kansas (Johnson County) Current
City Council override · reported July 25, 2026

KCUR's fresh reporting details how the Edgerton City Council unanimously overrode its own Planning Commission's rejection of Dubai-based DAMAC Digital's data center plans at the Logistics Park Kansas City site, despite sustained resident objections over water and land impacts. Neighbors have circulated a petition seeking a public vote on future data center approvals, arguing the council bypassed its own process.
