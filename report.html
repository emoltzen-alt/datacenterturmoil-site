Data Center Turmoil — Community Response Report

Reporting window: past 24 hours (July 14–15, 2026), with older items for background context. Compiled July 15, 2026.
National trends: The moratorium wave keeps widening — Lawrence, KS imposed a 24-month pause on data centers on July 14, joining St. Charles County, MO, St. Joseph, MO, Santa Fe County, NM, and Missoula County, MT in halting new projects within the past two weeks alone. Outright rejections are also landing: Prince William County, VA's Board of Supervisors killed the 2,000-acre "Dulles South" campus 8-0 after a five-hour hearing, echoing Tyler, TX's permit denial and Stokes County, NC's commissioners voting down a proposed moratorium (leaving the door open to development there instead). Litigation is a growing front, with residents suing over approvals in Sangamon County, IL and a judge letting an Oxmoor Valley, AL nuisance suit proceed. Across nearly every hearing, the same complaints recur: strain on water supplies, rising electricity bills, noise from cooling systems, and skepticism toward tax-abatement deals. Independent trackers cited this week put active local opposition groups above 800 across 49 states, with an estimated $130B in projects blocked or delayed so far in 2026.
