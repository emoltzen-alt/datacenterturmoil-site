Girard, Ohio Current
City Council · final vote · July 13, 2026

Council took a third-reading, final vote tonight on an emergency ordinance imposing a year-long ban on new AI data centers, giving the city time to study impacts on its general development plan and zoning rules. Because it carries an emergency clause, the moratorium takes effect immediately once Mayor Mark Zuppo signs it. Other Mahoning Valley communities are weighing similar freezes as developers eye the region.

Source: WFMJ — "Girard Council taking final vote on data center moratorium"
Indianapolis, Indiana Current
City-County Council, Metropolitan & Economic Development Committee · public hearing · July 13, 2026

Council President Maggie Lewis's proposed moratorium on new data center development went before the committee at a 5:30 p.m. public hearing in the City-County Building, giving councilors, the Hogsett administration, and residents a chance to weigh long-term impacts before any further zoning approvals. Already-approved projects (Metrobloks, Sabey, DC Blox) would be grandfathered in; if advanced, the measure heads to the full council in August.

Source: Mirror Indy — "Council leader to introduce data center moratorium"
St. Joseph, Missouri Current
City Council · work session · July 13, 2026

Council reviewed citywide data center zoning and land-use policy at a work session, weeks after landowner Capstone Technology Campus LLC withdrew its rezoning and annexation bid for a 130-acre, four-building data center campus at 6321 Pickett Road amid organized opposition from the "Say NO to Data Centers St. Joe" group. Residents had pressed the council on July 6 for a broader zoning-law review.

Source: News-Press NOW — "City Council to review data center zoning and land use policies at work session July 13"
Pocatello, Idaho Current
City Council · appeal scheduled · reported July 13, 2026

Coverage published today confirms Pocatello's City Council will hear a developer's appeal on July 16 of its May denial of a $2.6 billion data center proposal. The dispute is part of a wider wave of data center protests across Idaho and Utah centered on energy consumption.

Source: Utah Public Radio — "Pocatello is hearing an appeal on a denied data center"
San Marcos, Texas Current
State legal/political fallout · TV segment aired July 12, 2026

A Texas political-affairs segment aired July 12 detailed the aftermath of San Marcos passing the state's first municipal data center ban: state Sen. Paul Bettencourt (R–Houston) says he will challenge the ordinance as exceeding the city's legal authority under state law, while the ban has also become a flashpoint in the governor's race, with candidates on both sides shifting positions.
